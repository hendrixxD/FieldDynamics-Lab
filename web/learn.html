Satellite Constellations & Datasets Used

NASA POWER Agroclimatology
1981–2025 daily radiation, humidity, wind, and temperature for FAO-56 Penman-Monteith reference evapotranspiration (ET0) and heat stress tracking.
NASA SMAP SPL4SMGP V008
Root-zone soil moisture anomalies (0–100 cm depth, ~9 km native resolution, 2015–2025) monitoring crop flowering water deficits.
CHIRPS v2.0 Rainfall
0.05° high-resolution rainfall records driving the A2 agronomic onset rule and dry spell duration monitoring.
NASA GPM IMERG V07 & SRTM 30 m
95th percentile extreme storm rainfall combined with 30 m SRTM elevation slopes and iSDA erodibility K factors for topsoil erosion risk.
NASA MODIS MOD13Q1 V6.1
250 m 16-day NDVI masked to ESA WorldCover cropland to detect bare-field duration and vegetative vigor transitions.

Validation & Uncertainty Handling

Agricultural models must be held accountable to real smallholder conditions. FieldDynamics validates its models using spatial block GroupKFold and leave-one-year-out cross-validation:

  • FAO-56 Penman-Monteith ET0: Validated against published FAO-56 benchmarks.
  • Weight Perturbation Robustness: Rotation rankings are tested by perturbing weights ±20% over 200 iterations to flag close calls.
  • Confidence Tiering: Every LGA displays a transparent data confidence level (High, Medium, Low) based on cloud-filling and cropland fraction.
Local Expertise: Satellite observations guide long-term regional probability. Field application always requires the judgment of farmers and local KADA extension officers.
